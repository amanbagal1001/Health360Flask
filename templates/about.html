About Health360

Smart Healthcare Analytics: Patient Risk & Lifestyle Insights

Turning healthcare data into meaningful insights.

Health360 is an interactive healthcare analytics project that explores patient information and patterns related to lifestyle, health indicators and disease risk.

Through an interactive Tableau dashboard and a guided data story, the project makes it easier to explore information, compare groups and understand patterns in the dataset.

✚

Our focus

Presenting healthcare data in a clear, accessible and visual way.

Project objectives

01

Patient distribution

Explore patient counts and age-group patterns.

02

BMI & health risk

Compare BMI categories with heart disease indicators.

03

Lifestyle patterns

Study exercise, alcohol and smoking-related indicators.

04

Health metrics

Review blood pressure and other patient metrics.
