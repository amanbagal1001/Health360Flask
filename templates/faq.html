Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the Health360 analytics website.

Everything you need to get started.

Find out how to explore the dashboard and story.

What is Health360?＋

Health360 is a healthcare analytics project that presents patient and lifestyle insights through interactive Tableau visualizations.

How do I use the dashboard?＋

Open the Dashboard page and use the charts, filters and interactive controls inside the Tableau view.

What is the Story page?＋

The Story page presents a sequence of visualizations that guides you through selected insights from the healthcare dataset.

Why is Tableau not loading?＋

Check your internet connection and confirm that the Tableau Public workbook is published and publicly accessible.

Does this website store patient information?＋

No. This Flask website embeds the published Tableau views and does not collect or store patient data.
