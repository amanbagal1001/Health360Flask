QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

A few details to help you navigate the Health360 experience.

What is Health360?

Health360 is a healthcare analytics project developed by Aman Bagal that presents patient risk and lifestyle insights through interactive Tableau visualisations.

How do I use the dashboard?

Use the interactive controls inside the embedded Tableau view. Depending on the chart, you may be able to select categories, hover for details, or apply filters.

What is the Story page for?

The Story view groups visualisations into a sequence so you can explore different aspects of the dataset step by step.

Is this medical advice?

No. The website is an educational data visualisation project and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Why is a Tableau visualisation not loading?

Check your internet connection and make sure Tableau Public is accessible. You can also use the “Open in Tableau” link above each visualisation.
